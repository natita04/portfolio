How I lead

Leadership pillars

01

Impact customers feel

Design that ships and changes how people work every day. My team's latest release met platform standards, solved requests customers had waited years for, and laid the ground for AI-first workflows.

02

I step in when it counts

My role is strategy and leadership, but when a project is urgent and pivotal, I own it. I recently spent six months as lead designer on a major transformation and set the direction the team now builds on.

03

Growing people through ownership

I give designers big, visible problems before they feel ready. Then I protect their focus so they can do the best work of their careers.

04

AI as a working tool

I prototype with AI to put working experiences in front of stakeholders sooner, so decisions happen faster. I also bring my team along as we adopt new tools and workflows.
